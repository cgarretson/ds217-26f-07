The chart is a line graph depicting the percentage of patients meeting the exercise goal over the number of follow-up visits among patients the Center-based and Home-based programs. The x-axis shows follow-up visits 1-4, and the y-axis shows the percentage of patients seen at each visit who met the weekly goal of 150 minutes of exercise. The Home-based proportion who meet their goal increases from 58% at visit 1 to 70% at visit 4, while the Center-based proportion increases from 57% to 79%. At visit 4, Center-based is 9% higher than Home-based. Patients chose their program so the chart cannot show if one program caused more exercise. Each percentage also includes only the patients seen at that visit (about 45 per program) so the 9-point gap could reflect chance or differences in participants who returned.
